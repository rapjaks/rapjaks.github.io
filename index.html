About

I'm an industrial designer working at the intersection of human factors and spaceflight hardware. Since 2020 I've led design for crew systems and habitat interiors at Axiom Space, working alongside space architects, engineers, and astronauts to turn mission requirements into equipment people can trust in orbit.

Before that, I designed environmental control and crew systems for expandable habitats at Bigelow Aerospace under NASA's NextSTEP initiative, and spent four years at Owlet Baby Care building consumer hardware from prototype through mass manufacturing. I hold a BFA in Industrial Design from Brigham Young University.
